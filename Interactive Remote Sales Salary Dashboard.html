Spotlight: Negotiating the Base vs. Variable

1. Base Salary (The Safe Money)

This is your security blanket. For most Account Executive roles, the 50/50 split (Base = Variable) is the industry standard. Aim to maintain this parity.

2. Variable Commission (The Upside)

Be wary of roles with a very low base salary but an inflated OTE. This suggests high risk and an overly aggressive quota.

3. Sales Manager OTE

The manager OTE often falls slightly above the average AE OTE, but usually features a higher percentage of the total OTE in the Base salary.
