The Standard: Understanding the Remote AE 50/50 Salary Split

For key Account Executive roles (SMB, Mid-Market, and Enterprise), the 50/50 OTE split remains the established industry standard for remote sales compensation in 2026. This means your Base Salary and your at-quota Variable Commission are designed to be equal.

This model ensures security with the Base while providing massive upside through the Variable component. Knowing this split is crucial before you start your next salary negotiation—especially with companies adjusting comp structures post-market correction.

Exclusive Remote Sales Salary Negotiation Playbook for 2026

1. Base Salary Focus: Stability & Risk

Always aim to protect your Base Salary. A competitive base ensures financial security. If the proposed variable pay is disproportionately high, it can signal an extremely aggressive or unattainable quota. In 2026, expect bases to be 5-8% higher than 2025 benchmarks.

2. Variable Pay: Quota & Accelerators

The true value of the Variable component lies in the quota reality. Investigate the average attainment rates and accelerator tiers. An "inflated" OTE is worthless if the quota is unrealistic—ask for proof of rep attainment data from the past 4 quarters.

3. Sales Manager OTE Nuances

Sales Manager OTE is typically weighted more towards the Base Salary (often 60/40 or 70/30). Their variable pay is based on team performance, requiring less risk on individual performance. Expect manager bases to be $125k-$135k in 2026.
