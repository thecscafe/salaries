OTE Composition: Base vs. Variable

This chart breaks down the average On-Target Earnings (OTE) for each role, showing the split between stable Base Salary and performance-based Variable Commission. Notice how the variable portion grows significantly for closing roles (Account Executive).

Key Insight: The 50/50 Split

For most Account Executive roles (SMB, Mid-Market, and Enterprise), the **50/50 split** is the industry standard. This means your Base Salary and your at-quota Variable Commission are equal.

This structure is designed to provide a stable income (Base) while aggressively rewarding high-performers (Variable). When negotiating, aim to maintain this balance.

Your Negotiation Playbook

1. Base Salary (The Safe Money)

This is your security blanket. For most AE roles, the 50/50 split is standard. A low base relative to variable pay can be a red flag for high-risk, high-pressure quotas.

2. Variable (The Upside)

Don't just look at the OTE figure. Ask about quota, commission rates, and accelerators. An "inflated" OTE with an unrealistic quota isn't a real offer.

3. Sales Manager OTE

A manager's OTE is often slightly above the average AE's, but it's typically weighted more heavily towards the Base Salary to reflect leadership and team performance over individual sales.
