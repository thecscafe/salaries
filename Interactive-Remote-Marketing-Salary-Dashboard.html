The Standard: Understanding the Remote Marketing 85/15 Salary Split

For most marketing roles, the 85/15 OTE split is the established industry standard for remote marketing compensation. This means your Base Salary comprises the majority at 85%, with a 15% performance-based Bonus tied to pipeline generation, MQLs, and campaign performance.

This model ensures financial stability while rewarding measurable marketing impact. Some roles like Demand Gen may have a 80/20 split due to direct pipeline attribution.

Exclusive Remote Marketing Salary Negotiation Playbook

1. Base Salary Dominance

Marketing roles prioritize Base Salary (85%+ of OTE) because impact is measured across longer cycles. Unlike sales, your bonus is tied to pipeline influence, not closed deals. Negotiate base aggressively.

2. Bonus Metrics Clarity

Ensure your Performance Bonus (10-20% of OTE) is tied to metrics you control: MQLs, pipeline contribution, campaign ROI, content performance. Avoid bonuses tied solely to company revenue or sales quotas.

3. Specialized Role Premium

Product Marketing and Demand Gen roles command 15-25% higher compensation than general marketing managers due to direct revenue impact and specialized skills. Know your market value.
